Media Application

- Application information and rules -

1. You must answer all questions with correct information. Make sure your username and Discord name are written correctly. Otherwise, we will not be able to identify you.

2. Please be patient, as a response may take 24–48 hours. Do not expect to receive your rank immediately.

3. If you do not receive your role after a week, it likely means your answers were incorrect.

4. You must reapply every 30 days or you will lose your rank.

5. A video upload is required. Only posting your channel link is not allowed. It must be posted on YouTube or TikTok. Any unclear or unsafe links will result in instant rejection and punishment.

6. You must be in the EGOMC Discord server and also in the Content Creators Discord server.

7. The video must be yours. If you do not own the video, your application will be rejected.

8. You must meet all rank requirements.

9. The video must be recorded on EGOMC servers and uploaded recently. (We do not accept old content).

10. Please record the video with the correct aspect ratio and proper resolution.

11. You may only upload again for a higher rank if you previously applied for a lower rank.

12. When we mention "views", we mean the views on the video you submitted, not total channel views.

13. Asking about your application status will result in a warning, then rejection if repeated.

14. Using programs to boost views will result in a permanent ban from applying in the future.

- Video Requirements:

The video must be in 720p or higher and 60 FPS for the entire video.
The video must also be at least 3 minutes long.
If the video does not meet these standards, it will be instantly rejected.

Submitted videos must show real effort and provide entertaining and valuable content.
Videos that appear low effort will be rejected.
Reviewers will evaluate the video and then contact you via Discord to confirm if it meets the requirements.
