Staff Application

Please answer the following questions if you would like to join the EGOMC staff team. Once you submit your application, a staff manager will review it and decide whether you are suitable for the position.
If you are accepted, you will receive a message via Discord. If your application is rejected, you must wait 20 days before applying again.

Requirements:

- You must be 14 years old or older.
- You must have a valid and secure email.
- You must have at least 5 hours of playtime per week.
- You must be a member of the EGOMC Minecraft server for at least one week.
- You must be a member of our EGOMC Discord server for at least two weeks.
- You must have a microphone.
- You must be able to record high-quality videos, as you will need to record evidence of player activity.
- Previous staff experience is not required but is preferred.
- Any application generated using AI will result in rejection and may lead to a blacklist.

Notice:
When writing your application, please do not include personal information such as:

- Phone number (it is enough that you have a working phone).
- Your email address.
- Your home address.
